# OFT Racing Shop — recommended content map

This is an information-priority recommendation, not a wireframe. Claude Design may explore composition, but should preserve the hierarchy and evidence rules.

## Content principle

> The information architecture should adapt to what is actually useful for that specific business.

For OFT Racing, the PiriCard job is to help a tapped-in visitor identify the business, act quickly, understand Shop versus Workshop, and reach trustworthy practical information. It is not a full catalogue, ecommerce site, or campaign landing page.

## Level 0 — PiriCard product frame

- Shared PiriCard symbol + `PiriCard` wordmark.
- Route to the directory.
- Compact top-bar behavior with safe-area support.
- OFT orange may adapt the mark accent; the lockup itself stays recognizable.

## Level 1 — Immediately visible

### Business identity

- Authentic storefront/exterior hero **placeholder until an approved image exists**.
- Official OFT Racing logo **placeholder until the official file exists**.
- Name: **OFT Racing Shop**.
- Approved customer-facing category; use the supplied motorcycle-retailer category as the factual baseline.
- São Pedro da Cadeira.
- Live open/closed state plus today's hours.

### Highest-value actions

Recommended priority:

1. **Ligar** — supported by the confirmed phone.
2. **Como chegar** — supported by the confirmed address; exact Maps destination still needs verification.
3. **Guardar contacto** — PiriCard utility; organization/display details must be confirmed before production.
4. **Avaliações Google** — only after the direct review URL and current rating/count are verified.

Do not reserve visible slots for WhatsApp, email, website, Instagram, or Facebook until their exact destinations are confirmed. Once verified, Instagram may be more useful near the top than a lower-value generic action because social content is central to OFT's public communication.

## Level 2 — What OFT is

### Short overview

One factual, compact statement should explain the business without promotional invention. Final wording requires OFT approval.

### Two-part business model

Use two clear concepts:

- **SHOP** — motorcycles, products, equipment, and accessories; exact categories pending verification.
- **WORKSHOP** — mechanical/service side; exact capabilities pending verification.

For the design exploration, both labels must be marked as provisional content because only the retail category is formally confirmed and workshop detail comes from supplied social observation.

## Level 3 — Areas and culture, correctly classified

Do not render one undifferentiated “Services” list. Prepare separate containers or content types so approved information can later be classified as:

| Content type | Current signals | Publication status |
|---|---|---|
| Products/categories | Motorcycles; Moto 4/ATV; SSV; E-bikes; helmets; boots; goggles; equipment; accessories; apparel | Observed; confirm current range |
| Activities/disciplines | Enduro; Motocross; off-road; road motorcycles; racing/performance | Observed; useful as culture/context |
| Workshop services | Mechanical/workshop presence | Exact list unknown |
| Brands seen in communication | KTM; Husqvarna; CFMOTO; GASGAS | Observed only; no dealer claim |

Claude Design should allow these groups to have different visual treatments and labels. A brand strip should not be designed as “official brands” unless OFT supplies authorization and current relationships.

## Level 4 — Social proof

- Current research snapshot: **4.8/5, 35 Google reviews**.
- Label the research/design source as dated until launch verification.
- Provide a clear “Ler avaliações no Google” action only when the direct review URL is supplied.
- Do not invent testimonials, rating bars, star distributions, sentiment summaries, or review excerpts.

If the direct link is still missing in a design concept, use a clearly named placeholder such as `PLACEHOLDER — Google review URL`, not a clickable fake destination.

## Level 5 — Practical visit information

- Full weekly hours with Monday and Sunday shown as closed.
- Highlight today's row and show current Lisbon-time status.
- Full address.
- Map/directions action; an embedded map is optional, not part of core DNA.
- Phone in readable and tappable form.
- Note that holiday/special hours may differ if this warning is approved.

This section may repeat call and directions because they remain useful at the end of a longer profile.

## Level 6 — Verified social and digital utilities

When destinations are confirmed:

- Instagram.
- Facebook.
- Share PiriCard profile.
- Show/copy permanent QR/profile URL.
- Save vCard contact.

Do not show an official website action while the verified state is “no website associated”. Do not add email or WhatsApp until confirmed.

## Suggested omission rules

| Element | Show when | Otherwise |
|---|---|---|
| Call | Phone is valid | Omit |
| Directions | Address/map destination is verified | Omit until resolved |
| Google Reviews | Direct URL and fresh rating/count are verified | Omit action; placeholder only in design file |
| WhatsApp | OFT confirms a WhatsApp-enabled number | Omit |
| Email | OFT confirms the address | Omit |
| Website | OFT confirms an official website | Omit |
| Instagram/Facebook | Exact official URLs are confirmed | Omit clickable action |
| Brand logos | Current relationship and usage permission are confirmed | Use plain observed-content notes only, or omit |
| Workshop services | OFT supplies an approved list | Show only a neutral Workshop label pending approval |
| Gallery | Authentic approved photos exist | Omit; do not fill with stock |

## Content-length guidance

- Keep the top identity description to roughly 1–2 short sentences.
- Keep action labels direct: `Ligar`, `Como chegar`, `Avaliações`, `Instagram`, `Facebook`, `Guardar contacto`.
- Prefer categorized short lists over long paragraphs.
- Do not repeat the full business overview in later sections.
- Avoid generic claims such as “best service”, “unmatched performance”, or “everything for your bike”.
