# OFT Racing Shop — open questions

Missing answers are not blockers for design exploration. They are blockers only for publishing the affected content or action. Every unresolved item used in a design file must be labelled as a placeholder.

## Priority 1 — identity and rights

- What is the official current OFT Racing logo file? Prefer SVG, PDF, or transparent high-resolution PNG.
- Are there horizontal, stacked, monochrome, and small-size logo variants?
- What are the official color values and any brand usage restrictions?
- Which storefront/exterior photograph is preferred for the hero?
- Are there approved shop, workshop, racing, team/community, product, and equipment photos?
- Does OFT own or have permission to use each supplied social photograph in a public PiriCard profile?
- May manufacturer/product imagery and logos be used, and under what permissions?

## Priority 2 — essential destinations

- What is the exact Google Maps place/directions URL or place ID?
- What is the exact Google Reviews destination?
- What is the exact official Instagram URL/handle?
- What is the exact official Facebook page URL?
- Is 919 678 052 WhatsApp-enabled for customer contact?
- Is there an official business email?
- A third-party listing shows `oftracingshop.geral@gmail.com`; is it current and approved for publication?
- Is there now an official website, even though none was associated with the supplied Google Business snapshot?

## Priority 3 — business content

- Is **OFT Racing Shop** the approved display name and the organization/legal name for the vCard?
- What short customer-facing category should be used in Portuguese?
- Should the profile explicitly present **Shop** and **Workshop** as the two main areas?
- What is the complete, approved workshop service list?
- Which categories are currently sold: motorcycles, Moto 4/ATV, SSV, E-bikes, road bikes, equipment, accessories, apparel, or others?
- Which observed terms are activities/community topics rather than products or services?
- Which products or categories are most important after an NFC/QR tap?
- Are bookings required for workshop work, and if so, through which verified channel?

## Priority 4 — brand relationships

- Is OFT currently an official dealer, representative, service point, or reseller for CFMOTO?
- What is the current relationship with KTM, Husqvarna, and GASGAS?
- Does the Polisport store-locator entry reflect a current approved relationship?
- Which brand logos may legally appear, and can OFT provide approved originals?
- Should the public racing-team name be mentioned, and how does it relate to the retail business?

Until answered, use “observed in supplied social material” in research notes only. Do not use “official dealer”, “authorized”, “represented brands”, or equivalent public claims.

## Priority 5 — operational freshness

- Confirm all weekly hours immediately before launch.
- Are there lunch closures, seasonal changes, appointment-only periods, or holiday exceptions?
- Confirm the full postal address and postal code.
- Confirm the international phone format for tap-to-call and vCard.
- Refresh the Google rating and review count at launch.
- Decide whether dated rating data should be displayed at all if it cannot be maintained.

## Product/design decisions

- Should the primary action remain Call, or does OFT have another verified preferred contact flow?
- After verification, should Instagram be a top-level action because of OFT's active social communication?
- Is a map embed useful, or is a lighter directions link better for speed and focus?
- Is a photo gallery useful after authentic approved assets arrive?
- Should the digital-card preview appear, or should save/share/QR utilities be more compact?
- What desktop width/composition best supports the final content without weakening the mobile-first experience?
- Does OFT need Portuguese only, or a second language?
